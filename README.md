Motivation and Summary of Findings:



Research Question:

Field crews currently face daily exposure to underperforming and failing wells, with no clear guide to where their time is best spent. Visiting every well is inefficient and unnecessarily risky. We want to identify which producing wells are likely to fail (based on factors such as RPM, Torque, and other variables), and which of those are worth visiting first, so that crews go to the right wells, oil production is protected, and worker exposure to danger is minimized?
